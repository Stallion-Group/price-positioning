Notes and sources
  • Filters: choices within a group add together (OR) (Electric + Sedan shows every electric model and every sedan); the Segment, Type and Origin groups narrow each other (AND). A group with nothing selected does not filter.
  • Segments follow the common European classes, assigned by size and positioning: A city car; B small car and small SUV; C compact; D mid-size; E large or executive (includes Land Cruiser-class SUVs). The dataset’s own segment labels were mixed, so each model was classified individually.
  • Type: Small SUV means an A- or B-segment SUV; SUV means C segment and above. Electric (all) covers three powertrains, which can also be chosen on their own: BEV (battery-electric), EREV (extended-range electric: an electric drive with a petrol generator; here the Deepal G318, S05 and S07, whose 900–1,100 km quoted ranges are EREV figures) and PHEV (plug-in hybrid). Self-charging hybrids (Suzuki Across, Kia Niro, Toyota Urban Cruiser MHEV, Soueast S06 Hybrid, Hyundai Palisade Hybrid) are not counted as electric. Manual and Automatic come from each trim’s description; where none was given, automatic is assumed and marked with ✱ in the table.
  • Difference from median is each model’s entry price minus the median entry price of all models in its segment under the current filters (dashed line on each chart). Negative means cheaper than the typical model in the segment.
  • Prices: entry price (cheapest trim) is plotted; the faint bar runs to the dearest trim. Newer official lists replace dataset rows for MG (Stallion MG, 7 Oct 2026), Hyundai (Hyundai Motors Nigeria, 3 Sep 2026) and Geely (Africar, 4 Sep 2026); Bestune and Honda come from the dataset. MG ZS and HS and Geely Galaxy L6 and L7 are Minadi-distributed models from the dataset. Toyota combines TNL and CFAO prices.
  • Origin is the brand’s home country: Chinese includes MG (SAIC-owned) and Bestune (FAW); Korean is Hyundai and Kia; Japanese is Honda, Toyota, Suzuki, Nissan and Mitsubishi; Other is Ford and Jeep (US), Renault (France) and IVM (Nigeria). Choose Chinese, Korean or Japanese to compare our brands with rivals from one country only.
  • Bestune T77: there is no Bestune T70; the T77 is TBA on the Bestune list and is shown at an estimated price. A FAW-badged T77 appears in the dataset at ₦36.0M (May 2025) and is visible with pre-2026 prices switched on.
  • Pre-2026 prices (Ford, Renault, FAW, IVM, GWM Tank, Jeep) are hidden by default because they are 10–27 months old.
  • 5% pattern: many Chery, Changan, Maxus, BYD, GAC and Wuling prices are exactly 95% of a round figure, which suggests a 5% adjustment in how they were recorded. Check this before comparing them directly with official lists.

* Prices from public internet sources
